# Code of conduct

Help make this project welcoming and useful to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and constructive. Discuss the code, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish someone else's private information.
- Assume questions and corrections are made in good faith, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Keep issues and reviews relevant to the project. Give maintainers and contributors reasonable time to respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing so would not expose private information.

During private development, report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through your established contact channel. A public reporting channel will be documented before public launch. Do not post sensitive personal details in an issue.
